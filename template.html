Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance (daily closes, adjusted). Entry = close on the mention day (or next trading day). Pre windows (-30d, -7d) end at the prior close; "day 0" = prior close → mention-day close (the reaction; for after-hours remarks the reaction shows up in +1d).
