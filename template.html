Data: Yahoo Finance via yfinance (daily closes, split/dividend-adjusted). Entry = close on the mention day (or next trading day/first available close). Horizons are calendar days (±1d = adjacent trading close). Because entry is the mention-day close, the -7d and -1d windows include the mention-day reaction itself (e.g. SPY +10.5% on Apr 9 2025), so they measure "run-up into the close", not purely pre-announcement moves. Benchmark: SPY for stocks, BTC for crypto. "Beat" = return above benchmark over the same window. Includes direct "buy" calls plus company/product praise and family-linked ventures; classification shown in the Category column. Not investment advice; small sample, heavy overlap of crypto names on single dates.
